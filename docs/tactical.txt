EpyScenario — Pertussis parameters: tactical "how to derive from local data" guidance
=======================================================================================

This file holds the operational recipes for estimating each pertussis model
parameter from locally-monitored surveillance data. It was moved out of the
in-app parameter tooltips (which now carry Description + Default rationale) so the
tooltips stay concise; the same recipes also drive the interactive calculators on
the Model References page. Keep this in sync with schemas.py (SEIRS Pertussis)
and pages/Model_References calculators.

R0 — Basic reproduction number
  How to derive: fit to the early exponential growth rate r of local case counts,
    R0 ≈ 1 + r·D, where D is the generation interval (≈ incubation + infectious
    period ≈ 30 days). Prefer a full ABC-SMC calibration to the weekly curve — the
    1+r·D shortcut understates R0 for a long-generation pathogen like pertussis.
  Tactical: from two early weekly case counts C1 then C2 on the rising limb,
    r = ln(C2/C1)/(days between) per day, so R0 ≈ 1 + r×30.

Incubation period (1/epsilon)
  How to derive: CDC/clinical references, or contact-tracing data (mean
    exposure-to-symptom-onset interval).
  Tactical: average of (cough-onset date − exposure date) across investigated
    cases with a single identifiable exposure.

Infectious period — naive (1/gamma)
  How to derive: communicability guidance, or the observed serial interval minus
    the latent period.
  Tactical: median of (effective-treatment-start date − cough-onset date) + 5 days,
    capped at ~21 days if untreated.

Infectious period — partial (1/gamma_p)
  How to derive: studies of vaccinated / previously-infected cases, or set as a
    fraction of the naive infectious period (must stay <= naive).
  Tactical: the same onset-to-treatment calculation, computed only over up-to-date
    (vaccinated) cases in the line list.

Relative infectiousness of Ip (sigma)
  How to derive: secondary-attack-rate studies comparing vaccinated vs
    unvaccinated index cases (typically 0.1–0.5). Primary calibration target.
  Tactical: sigma = (secondary cases per contact of vaccinated index cases) ÷
    (secondary cases per contact of unvaccinated index cases), from contact
    investigations.

Relative susceptibility of Sp (delta)
  How to derive: vaccine-effectiveness studies; delta ≈ 1 − VE against infection
    (typically 0.15–0.30).
  Tactical: delta = 1 − VE, using the screening-method
    VE = 1 − [PCV/(1−PCV)]·[(1−PPV)/PPV], where PCV = % of cases vaccinated
    (line list) and PPV = population coverage (immunization registry / IIS).

Waning full->partial (omega_1, years)
  How to derive: cohort/serological reinfection-interval studies, or fit to the
    inter-epidemic (surge-to-surge) period in local case series.
  Tactical: 1/omega_1 ≈ the average number of years between successive pertussis
    episodes in repeat patients, or the observed interval between local surges.

Waning partial->susceptible (omega_2, years)
  How to derive: long-term reinfection / serology studies; often set as the
    estimated total duration of immunity minus the omega_1 phase.
  Tactical: if no local reinfection data, set 1/omega_2 ≈ (assumed total years of
    immunity) − (omega_1 years), e.g. 16 − 4 ≈ 12 y.

Waning vaccine Sp->S (omega_3, years)
  How to derive: vaccine-effectiveness-over-time studies (the annual decline in VE
    after the primary series).
  Tactical: stratify cases by years since last dose, compute screening-method VE in
    each stratum, and read off the years for VE to fall to about half its initial
    value (VE half-life: t½ = t·ln2 / ln(VE0/VEt)).

Seasonality peak day
  How to derive: the month-of-onset distribution in local surveillance — take the
    peak month's mid-point as day-of-year; or use the app's multi-year NNDSS
    seasonality estimator.
  Tactical: peak month of monthly case counts → day ≈ (month−1)×30 + 15.

Seasonality amplitude
  How to derive: the peak-to-trough amplitude of de-trended (seasonally decomposed)
    local case counts.
  Tactical: compute (peak-month − trough-month cases) ÷ mean monthly cases — a
    small ratio maps to 'Low', a large one to 'Strong'.
